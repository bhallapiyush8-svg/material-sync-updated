The Challenge
Unstandardized Catalogs & Idle Spares

Each CPSE maintains independent ERP item master codes (SAP MM, Oracle EBS). Because naming is unstandardized, one public enterprise often floats costly tenders or import orders for valves, fasteners, and flanges, while an identical or standard-equivalent spare sits idle in another CPSE's warehouse just a few hundred kilometers away.

The Solution
Common Material Identity & Reallocation

MaterialSync parses engineering specifications across CPSE catalogs, verifies physical compatibility under statutory codes (DIN 933, ASME B16.34, NACE MR0175), and enables direct inter-CPSE stock transfers—freeing up locked working capital and cutting procurement lead time from 14 weeks to 3 days.

Standard Operating Framework • 3 Core Stages
Stage 01
Specification Normalization

Standardizes metallurgy, thread, pressure rating, and dimensions (DIN, ISO, ASME, IS) across CPSE catalogs.

Stage 02
Inter-CPSE Inventory Transfer

Pairs donor CPSE surplus with recipient procurement indents; coordinates CONCOR dedicated rail freight.

Stage 03
Gazetted Officer Sign-off

Class-I Materials Managers review and sign transfer indents with permanent SHA-256 CAG audit lineage.
