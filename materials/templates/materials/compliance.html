Why AI similarity alone isn't enough

A generic AI model will rate an "SS304 Ball Valve" and an "SS316 Ball Valve" as 94%+ similar because the text looks nearly identical. But substituting SS304 into an ONGC offshore sour gas pipeline causes hydrogen-induced sulfide stress cracking (SSC) within weeks — a catastrophic failure.

MaterialSync pairs AI text matching with this compliance twin so no inventory transfer quietly bypasses a safety standard.
